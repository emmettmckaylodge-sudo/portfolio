Stop 20

Alright, big jump here (Ahahaha foreshadowing!). I arrived in Moab Utah one day after catastrophic flash flooding hit the region. This road is called Kanes Creek, and it follows the Colorado River through Utah. I'm standing on the road on one side, looking across to campers who have been stranded by the floods. The road is gone and they were just waiting for the water to lower so they could try fording it. There was nothing I could do to help so I turned around.

Stop 21

Stop 41

Big fast forward again, this is in Vail Colorado - a high elevation area known for its world class ski slopes. I wanted to ascend a peak near vail called "The Spider", which would get me to 12,500 feet of altitude. I got up early in the morning and started climbing the unfamiliar roads in the dark, with just my headlights and the moon to keep me from falling off the edge.

Stop 48

You might be wondering how I find these trails and roads. I use software that I pay for called OnX, it's specifically designed for recreational explorers, hikers, 4x4'ers, and others. It lets explorers report on the conditions they find, it provides critical information such as trail length and elevation, and has advanced GIS mapping capabilities that show private land parcels and other property borders so you don't end up tresspassing and can stay on public land.
